Two gaps you should know about before using this.

Live hiring status is not verified per company. Confirming "currently hiring" for 113 companies means opening 113 careers pages and job boards on the same day, and that check expires within about a week. Rather than print a status column that would be stale or guessed, every row links to a live careers search and a LinkedIn Jaipur jobs search for that exact company. Those return today's real answer in one click. The companies where a specific Jaipur posting was actually seen during this research are marked with evidence code D.

Named HR contacts, personal emails and phone numbers are not included. Not because they are unfindable, but because assembling a few hundred individual recruiters' names, inboxes and numbers into one file is a different thing from helping you apply — and any that could not be sourced would have to be invented, which you explicitly ruled out. The reliable method for finding the right person yourself, one company at a time, is at the end.

How to read the evidence column

AConfirmed campus in Jaipur
Named in Mahindra World City / Rajasthan SEZ records or the company's own press material as operating a Jaipur facility. The strongest evidence here.
BJaipur-headquartered, multiple sources
Appears as a Jaipur company across independent listings, often with a street address in Sitapura, Malviya Nagar, Mansarovar or Vaishali Nagar.
CVetted agency directory
Carries a "verified company, Jaipur, India" listing on TechBehemoths or a top-reviewed placement on The Manifest, both of which check location.
DJaipur job posting seen
A role with a Jaipur (or named Jaipur neighbourhood) location appeared on Indeed or Glassdoor during this research. These are the rows with live hiring signal.
EJaipur-HQ company in funding data
Listed with a Jaipur headquarters in Inc42's funding database, with employee counts as shown in the notes.

Where a row shows more than one code, more than one independent source put the company in Jaipur. Rows resting on a single C or a single B are the ones to sanity-check first.

Finding the right recruiter yourself

This is the part deliberately left out of the table above. Done per company, at the moment you are ready to write to them, it takes about three minutes and gives you a current name rather than a stale one.

  1. Open the company's LinkedIn page from the table, click People, and filter by the keyword recruiter, talent or HR. Jaipur-based names surface at the top.
  2. Cross-check against who actually posted the job. On most LinkedIn listings the poster's name and photo sit under the job title — that person is the one reading applications, and is a better target than a generic HR head.
  3. For smaller companies in Tier 3, skip HR entirely and find the CTO or engineering lead. At under fifty people they hire directly.
  4. For the company's official address, use the contact page on its own website rather than a directory. Aggregator sites carry a lot of dead numbers — the 2011-era Jaipur IT directories still circulating are full of them, which is why none were used here.
  5. Send a connection request with a note, not a cold InMail. A short message naming one specific thing the company builds outperforms a CV attachment by a wide margin.

What was left out, and why

Companies that only show Jaipur because a remote listing is visible there were excluded, as were Delhi, Gurgaon, Noida, Bangalore, Pune, Hyderabad and Mumbai firms with no Jaipur office. Pure digital-marketing and SEO agencies were dropped even when Jaipur-verified, since they do not hire software engineers. Several names circulating on older Jaipur IT directories were also dropped: those lists date to around 2011 and their phone numbers and contacts no longer resolve.

Two entries deserve a specific caveat. DealShare was founded in Jaipur but is now headquartered in Bengaluru, so it is not in the list. Tata Consultancy Services is included on a lower grade of evidence than the other large employers — its Jaipur presence is widely reported but was not confirmed here against a primary source, so verify the delivery-centre address before you count on it.
